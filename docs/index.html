What counts as progress?

Evidence
A source tied to an exact claim.
Experiment
A comparison with a rule that can reject the idea.
Result
An observed outcome someone else can check.
Correction
A visible revision that preserves what changed.

These four questions are curator-proposed studies. No general resolution or community replication is claimed.

A shared notebook for things we can check

Questions stay attached to their sources, assumptions, proposed checks, and unresolved limits. Small contributions and negative results are welcome. A problem stays open until evidence meets its stated acceptance rule. A failed check narrows a claim to the conditions tested.
